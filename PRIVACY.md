# Privacy — Primbon Jawa & Calc Civil

This desktop edition does not include Quran, hadith, Al-Hikam, prayer, salawat, Maulid reading, audio, or article datasets whose redistribution rights have not been verified.

## Data that stays on the device

Local calculation features do not require an account. Preferences and some settings may be stored in the application's local storage.

The Qibla azimuth calculation uses the device location or manually entered coordinates and runs locally. This desktop edition does not read orientation sensors.

## Optional connections

When related features are used, the application may send requests to BMKG, Open-Meteo, OpenStreetMap/Nominatim, exchange-rate or market providers, map or embedded-media services, and Pollinations AI. A location or coordinates may be sent for weather or location searches. Prompts and conversation text sent to AI features are processed by those providers and leave the device.

The in-app browser loads websites requested by the user in a window belonging to the software. A visited website may receive the IP address, requested page address, site cookies, and other data according to the user's interactions. Browser cookies and cache are stored in a dedicated application session. Site permissions such as location, camera, and microphone are denied in the browser window. HTTP/HTTPS links from the application and page pop-ups are directed to the software's browser window without opening an external browser.

External providers may receive IP addresses and request metadata and process them under their own policies. Use of online features is optional; each provider's policies and terms apply.
